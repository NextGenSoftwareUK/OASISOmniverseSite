Omniverse Game Runtime

OGEngine is the runtime that connects every OASIS world into a single interoperable omniverse. It provides shared state, inventory management, quest handling and karma propagation across all connected games and metaverses in real-time.

Build your own world, connect it to shared STAR quests, and give players real reasons to stay engaged across the whole ecosystem — not just one game.

StarNet is the publishing and distribution layer for the OASIS omniverse. Developers publish OAPPs and digital assets. Players discover, acquire and use them across every connected world — with no platform lock-in.

Holons are composable digital building blocks — reusable templates that accelerate creation and allow assets to flow freely throughout the ecosystem.

HOW IT WORKS
01
HOLONIC DATA
Identity-first data objects persist across providers instead of being locked to one chain, database or app.
02
HYPERDRIVE ROUTING
Auto-failover, auto-replication, load balancing and provider selection route operations across the strongest available path.
03
STAR CREATION LAYER
Builders can create OAPPs, quests, missions, NFTs, GeoNFTs, runtimes and templates for the OASIS Omniverse.
04
COSMIC ORM
A single data abstraction layer targets Web2 databases, Web3 chains, files, cloud, IPFS and local storage without rewrites.
05
STARNET APP/ASSET STORE
The STAR API & STAR CLI/ODK includes the STARNET App/Asset store — publish, search, download, install and version-control apps & assets with advanced sandbox and side-by-side versioning.
06
SMART BRICKS
Web5 STARNET Holons wrap Web4 Holons, plugged together like lego into infinitely nestable components — Geo-NFTs, NFTs, OAPPs, Runtimes, Plugins, Zomes, Geo-HotSpots, Celestial Bodies and more.
07
UNIVERSAL IDENTITY & AVATAR
A single decentralised DID and avatar travels across every OAPP, chain and platform. Karma, inventory and progression are unified — log in once, carry everything everywhere.
08
CROSS-CHAIN NFT STANDARD
OASIS NFTs wrap tokens from multiple chains under one universal standard. Geo-NFTs can be pinned to real-world locations for AR/VR experiences — managed from a single dashboard.
09
ONET DECENTRALISED NETWORK
A self-organising P2P networking layer with multi-protocol discovery, Dijkstra/A* routing, distributed consensus and quantum-resistant encryption — architecturally impossible to shut down.
10
UNIVERSAL WALLET
One dashboard spans 50+ blockchain networks with real-time portfolio aggregation, one-click cross-chain transfers, DeFi protocol integration and seamless fiat on/off ramps.
11
KARMA & MULTI-TOKEN ECONOMY
A universal reputation layer tracks contributions across all platforms and rewards them with HERZ utility tokens, CASA governance tokens and HoloFuel resource credits.
